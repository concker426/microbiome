grouped by exact genus-sequence hash (identical inputs never cross folds).

\textbf{Key findings}: SimpleEmb+MLP uniquely handles healthy samples that confuse both
tree and Transformer models, and extreme low-richness IBD (4 tokens) where MGM fails.
HGB recovers borderline disease cases that SimpleEmb misses. The 1-token false positive
(degenerate profile) fools all models; extreme low-richness samples (Cluster~1 subtype)
are a systematic failure mode.
